3. What Randomica does

Randomica offers eight generators of random results:

  • Heads or Tails: tosses a coin that lands on heads or tails.
  • Dice: rolls up to ten dice with 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 20 sides and the d100 (a pair of 10-sided dice, tens and units), and shows each value and the total, with an optional modifier.
  • Answer Orb: answers a question with one of twenty phrases.
  • Crystal Ball: picks one of the options you type; if you ask it to, it leaves the option that came up out of the following rounds until you start again.
  • Number Draw: draws one or more whole numbers from a range you choose; if you ask it to, it does not repeat the ones already drawn until you start again.
  • Spin the Wheel: spins a wheel divided into slices for the options you type and stops on one of them.
  • Draw a Card: draws cards from a shuffled 52-card deck (plus two jokers, if you want them); the cards drawn do not go back into the deck until you shuffle it again.
  • Teams & Turns: using the names you type, picks one person, puts everyone in order or forms teams whose sizes differ by at most one person.

Note. The results are random: the Answer Orb's answers and the Crystal Ball's picks are not predictions or advice, and no result tells you what is best for you to do.

History and lists. The app keeps on your phone the last 200 results of each generator and the lists of options and names you save, each with at most 100 entries.
